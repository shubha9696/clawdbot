**Google Gemini (FREE):**

2. Sign in with Google account

**Telegram Bot (FREE):**
1. Open Telegram app
2. Search: `@BotFather`
3. Send: `/newbot`

## 💡 IMPORTANT NOTES

### About FREE Hosting:
- **Render FREE tier**: Service sleeps after 15 min of no activity
- **Wakes up**: Automatically in ~30 seconds when you message it
- **Workaround**: Use UptimeRobot.com (also FREE) to ping it every 5 min
- **Result**: Effectively 24/7 uptime for FREE!

### About AI Costs:
- **Google Gemini FREE tier**: 60 requests/minute
- **Typical usage**: 10-50 requests/day for job applications
- **Cost**: $0 if you stay in free tier
- **Upgrade**: Only if you need more (still cheap: ~$3-10/month)

### About Browser Automation:
- **Your PC must be on** for browser automation to work
- **Extension runs locally** on your browser
- **Gateway can be online** (Render) or local (your PC)
- **You can watch** everything it does in real-time
